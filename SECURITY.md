# Security

Lithos runs assistants and a background service on your computer. Setup and team launches may change configuration files and grant assistants access to tools. The [setup reference](docs/reference/getting-started.md#before-you-start) explains these changes.

## Report a problem

Please do not publish security details in an issue. Visit the [repository security page](https://github.com/amarionfred/workflow/security) and use **Report a vulnerability** if it is available.

If private reporting is unavailable, open an issue asking for a private contact without including the security details, logs, credentials, or personal information. Once a private channel is available, share the Lithos version, what happened, and steps to reproduce it.

Lithos is intended for your own computer or a trusted private network. Keep your installation and assistants' permissions appropriate to your environment.
