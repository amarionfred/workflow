# Getting started with Lithos

Lithos helps a team of AI assistants work together on your project. You give the team a goal, and its operator helps you choose the right team and follow its progress.

## What you need

You need a Mac or Linux computer, Node.js 22 or 24, Git, tmux, and a signed-in Codex or Claude Code account. You also need a project folder.

## Install and start

Follow the [getting-started guide](lithos-getting-started.md) to install Lithos from this repository, preview setup, and open the operator. If you are new to the terminal, someone familiar with it can help.

Tell the operator what you want to do and where your project is. Start with a small, clear request. You decide what to approve and can review the work as it progresses.

## A few useful words

- **Operator:** the assistant you talk to first.
- **Team:** assistants working together on your project.
- **Seat:** one assistant’s role in a team.
- **Dashboard:** a view of your teams and their progress.

For more details, see [technical help](help.md).
